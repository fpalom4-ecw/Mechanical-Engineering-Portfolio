Lunabotics Research

As part of the NASA Lunabotics team, I researched electromechanical components to identify the best options for our rover's actuation systems. This involved running force and geometry calculations in MATLAB to determine the most efficient locations to mount our actuators, balancing mechanical advantage against space and structural constraints on the robot.

Voice-Guided Scene Assistant (Accessibility Extension)

Building on my vision-language research, I'm working to extend the system into a Voice-Guided Scene Assistant, an accessibility-focused tool that combines the vision-language model with Parrot software to help disabled individuals navigate real world environments. Rather than requiring a screen or manual input, a user could simply ask aloud, such as "find the curb" or "where is the curb," and the system would use the live camera feed to visually locate the object and respond in real time, aiming to make independent navigation more accessible and intuitive.
