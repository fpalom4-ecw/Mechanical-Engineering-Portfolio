Loft Bed
Personal Project

Over time I've designed and built several loft beds at increasing heights, with my biggest one:

9 ft tall with full safety rails.

It's got 6 ft of clearance underneath for a queen mattress, plus an extra foot up top for storage.

Since I could walk fully underneath it, it freed up a good chunk of usable floor space, enough to fit a desk and drawers under there too.

For this loft, I also built a matching cat wall system: a two-chamber cat house that holds up to three cats, ledges so they can walk across the wall, and two bridge shelves connecting straight up to the bed.

The project pulled together structural safety planning, tight space design, and just a lot of hands-on carpentry.
